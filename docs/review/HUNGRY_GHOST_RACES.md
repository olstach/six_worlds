The outer obscuration, and the walkers of the preta realm. The newly dead of the hungry ghost realm. Rolangs have been in the world of the dead for a relatively short time and are the least conscious of its inhabitants. The predators of the underworld, the shaza aggressively seek out bodies to devour.
